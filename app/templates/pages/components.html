Components

A title, a description under it, an image gallery with as many columns as it's given, a table, parts side by side, a card holding any of them (its title, description and footer each optional), and a divider between parts. Then blocks after shadcn/ui, made of the same parts plus stats, items, progress bars and charts. Then the parts of the app's own pages: cards with a cover, image tiles, a filter bar, thumbnails, drop zones, a form, dialogs, badges, empty states, History as a table and a link list.
